Learn about Cybersecurity, IT, and Threats

Module 1 - General Security Concepts (16%)

Compare and contrast various types of security controls: Understand how layered security controls work together to protect systems and data. Demonstrate the impact of change management processes on security: Understand how planning, testing, approvals, and documentation help maintain secure environments. Explain the importance of using appropriate cryptographic solutions: Apply encryption, certificates, hashing, digital signatures, and key management practices.

Module 2 - Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Attacks (24%)

Compare and contrast common threat actors and motivations: Understand who conducts attacks and why organizations are targeted. Explain common threat vectors and attack surfaces: Identify how attacks target users, systems, applications, networks, cloud resources, and supply chains. Analyze indicators of malicious activity: Recognize signs associated with malware, social engineering, credential attacks, and other security threats. Examine common vulnerabilities and associated risks: Evaluate vulnerabilities and help determine mitigation priorities. Explain the security risks associated with artificial intelligence (AI): Identify risks related to AI systems, models, prompts, privacy, and data exposure.

Module 3 - Security Architecture (19%)

Compare and contrast security implications of different architecture models: Understand security considerations across cloud, hybrid, on-premises, and operational technology environments. Apply security principles to secure enterprise infrastructure: Support secure system design, access controls, segmentation, and connectivity. Understand concepts related to data protection: Protect sensitive information throughout its lifecycle. Explain the importance of resilience and recovery in security architectures: Support continuity through backups, redundancy, recovery planning, and testing.

Module 4 - Security Operations (27%)

Apply common security techniques to computing resources: Help secure endpoints, networks, applications, and data. Explain asset management concepts and practices: Manage hardware, software, and data assets throughout their lifecycle. Analyze indicators of vulnerabilities and implement appropriate mitigation strategies: Identify, prioritize, remediate, and validate vulnerabilities. Describe security monitoring and alerting concepts: Use logs, alerts, dashboards, and security tools to identify potential threats. Manage identity and access controls: Support authentication, authorization, federation, and privileged access management. Explain the importance of automation and orchestration in security operations: Improve efficiency through automation, integrations, and AI-assisted workflows. Implement incident response activities: Support preparation, detection, containment, recovery, and lessons learned. Use data sources to support investigations: Analyze evidence and security data during investigations.

Module 5 - Security Program Management and Oversight (14%)

Define elements of an effective security governance program: Apply policies, standards, procedures, and security guidance. Manage security risk management processes and concepts: Assess, communicate, and manage organizational risk. Explain third-party risk management concepts: Evaluate and monitor vendor and partner security risks. Support security compliance and privacy considerations: Support regulatory, legal, compliance, and privacy requirements. Compare and contrast audits and assessments: Participate in audits, reviews, testing, and assessments. Promote security awareness practices: Help strengthen organizational security through education and awareness.
